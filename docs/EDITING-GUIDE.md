# How to update your website

You can change any detail on your website yourself, at any time. There is nothing to install and no code to touch. You type into labelled boxes and click one button.

## What you need

- The address of your editing page. It is your website address followed by `/admin`, for example `https://www.yourname.com/admin/`
- Your GitHub login (the free account you created for this)

Save the editing page as a bookmark so it is always one click away.

## Making a change

1. Open your editing page and click **Login with GitHub**.
2. On the left, click **Website content**. You will see eight sections, numbered 1 to 8.
3. Click the section you want to change. Each box is labelled, and the small grey text under a label explains what to type.
4. Change the text.
5. Click **Publish**, then **Publish now**.
6. Wait one to two minutes, then refresh your website. The change is live.

Only you and the people you have invited can log in. Visitors can only read the website.

## Which section changes what

| To change this | Open this section |
| --- | --- |
| Your name, title, phone, email, WhatsApp, address, social links, photos | **1. Contact details and home page** |
| Your biography, education, admissions, memberships, languages | **2. About her** |
| The legal services you offer and how you work with clients | **3. Practice areas** |
| Your jobs and experience | **4. Career background** |
| Cases you can talk about publicly, awards, articles | **5. Notable matters** |
| The wording of the contact section and the enquiry form choices | **6. Contact section and enquiry form** |
| The two large quote banners | **7. Quote banners** |
| The legal notice and disclaimer | **8. Legal notice and disclaimer** |

## Common tasks

**Change your phone number or email**
Open section 1. Update the boxes and publish. Every link and button on the website updates automatically.

**Type your WhatsApp number correctly**
Use digits only, with your country code first and no plus sign, spaces or dashes. For the number +44 7700 900123, type `447700900123`.

**Add or change your photo**
Open section 1, find **Portrait photo** and click it to upload a picture. A tall photo works best. The **Home page background photos** box shows up to three wide pictures behind your name.

**Add a new service or case**
Open the section, scroll to the list and click **Add** at the bottom. Fill in the boxes. To remove one, use the delete button beside it. To change the order, drag the item by its handle.

**Say you are not taking new clients**
In section 1, switch off **Show the availability badge**, then publish.

**Hide a social media link**
Delete the address in that box and publish. The icon disappears from the website.

## Good to know

- **Nothing goes live until you click Publish.** Closing the page without publishing simply discards your changes.
- **Every change is saved in the history.** If you make a mistake, tell the person who built the website and they can restore the earlier version.
- **Check your work.** After publishing, open the website on your phone and look at the part you changed.
- **Cases must be safe to share.** Only add notable matters your clients have approved, and leave out anything that could identify them.
- **Legal notice.** Ask your professional body or a colleague to review any changes to section 8.
- **Please do not share your login.** If someone else needs access, ask for them to be invited so they use their own account.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You cannot log in | Check you are using the GitHub account that was invited. Look for the invitation email and accept it |
| The change does not appear on the website | Wait two minutes, then refresh. On a phone, close the page and open it again |
| An error appears when you click Publish | Take a screenshot and send it to the person who built the website |
